# Herdeiros de Khan: Rules (English)

This file is an English version of the Brazilian Portuguese rulebook in `regras.pdf`. Page numbers refer to the booklet pages printed on the rulebook.

Some text in the rulebook is only in pictures. Items marked **[unclear]** come from a picture or are not in the rulebook. Make sure of them with the physical game or with the professor.

- Players: 2 to 5
- Age: 12 and up
- Time: 60 to 90 minutes

## 1. Story and goal (p. 2)

Genghis Khan is old, and the Mongols must choose his next leader. Each player is one of his heirs.

The goal is to get votes in the Kurultai council. To get votes, you:

- ride across Asia and collect tributes,
- attack cities and take their treasures,
- give the treasures to counselors,
- build yurts along your path.

When the Kurultai council is full, the player with the most votes becomes the next Khan.

## 4. The main board (p. 5)

The map shows the Mongol Empire in 3 regions: Russia, China, and Persia. Karakorum is in the center.

- **Routes and stops:** Your pawn moves along the routes, from stop to stop.
- **Cities:** Cities hold the treasures. There are always 3 cities open for attack at the same time. When a player conquers a city, a new city opens.
- **Provinces:** Provinces are the areas between the routes. You collect tribute pieces (yurts, swords, coins) from them.
- **Khan provinces:** Each time you use the Khan, you put him in a Khan province or in the upgrade area. In a Khan province, add 1 tribute piece to it and to its 2 adjacent provinces. Arrows from the Khan icon show these provinces.
- **Counselors:** Each double stop has one counselor. Give treasures to a counselor to get 1 vote for each delivery.
- **Upgrades:** Buy upgrades from this area to improve the actions on your player board.
- **Bonus pieces:** The first player who gives treasures to a counselor gets the bonus piece of that counselor. A bonus piece works one time only.
- **Karakorum:** Give the 3 specific treasures that Karakorum shows, and get 2 votes.
- **Kurultai track:** Each time you get a vote, move your token 1 space on this track.

City names on the map and on the city pieces include Kiev, Moscou, Sarai, Samarcanda, Bagdá, Kaesong, and Cabul. **[unclear]** The rulebook has no full list of cities, stops, provinces, or routes. Copy the map from the main board.

## 5. Setup (p. 3 to 4)

1. Shuffle each of these sets, one at a time: city pieces, treasures, open counselor cards, secret counselor cards, upgrades, bonus pieces.
2. Each player chooses a color and gets:
   - the player board of that color,
   - the 4 activation pieces of that color,
   - the 12 yurts of that color,
   - 2 secret counselor cards,
   - coins: 1 coin for the 1st and 2nd players, and 2 coins for each other player.
3. Put the main board in the center of the table.
4. Show 3 city pieces. Put the other city pieces in a pile near the board.
5. For each open city, show 4 treasures and put them on that city. Put the other treasures face down near the board.
6. Put the pawns of all players on Karakorum.
7. Optional: put the Karakorum piece on top of the Karakorum space on the board. This changes the treasures that Karakorum asks for.
8. Put 1 bonus piece at random on each of the 7 bonus symbols, Karakorum included.
9. Show 4 open counselor cards next to the board. Do not use the other cards in this game.
10. Put 1 tribute piece in each province, as the symbol on the board shows. Put the other tribute pieces face up near the board.
11. Put the Khan pawn next to the board, not on the map.
12. Put the player tokens and the neutral token on the seat icon next to the Kurultai track.
13. Show 5 upgrades and put them in the upgrade area at the bottom of the board. Put the other upgrades face down near the board.

## 6. Game flow (p. 6)

### Before the first round

Look at your 2 secret counselor cards. Keep 1 card and discard the other. The card you keep can give you votes at the end of the game.

### Round order

The player with the most descendants starts. Then play continues clockwise until the end of the game.

### Your turn

1. Put one of your activation pieces in one column of your player board.
2. Do the actions of that column.

- Each icon in the column is one action. Do the actions in any order.
- The activation piece stays in the column. You cannot use that column again until you get the piece back.
- The white column gives back all activation pieces.
- During the game, you can buy upgrades and add actions to a column.

Base actions of each column, from the picture of the Tolui board **[unclear]**:

| Column | Base icons |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 horse, 1 fist, 1 more icon |
| 2 | 2 horses, 1 fist |
| 3 | 1 horse, 2 fists, Khan |
| 4 (white) | 2 horses, Khan, recover |

The rulebook does not name the color of each column. Copy the base actions from a real player board.

## 7. Actions in your turn (p. 7 to 9)

### 7.1 Move

- The number of horses in the column is the number of moves.
- One move takes your pawn to an adjacent stop.
- You can skip your own yurts when you move. This is true even if another player is on that yurt.
- You can go through a stop with another pawn on it. But you cannot end your movement there. Exceptions: a double stop (2 pawns) and Karakorum (all pawns).
- Going through a double stop costs only 1 move.
- You can stop on, or go through, a yurt of another player.

### 7.2 Take tribute pieces

- The number of fists in the column is the number of tribute pieces you can take.
- Take tribute pieces from the provinces adjacent to each stop where you were during this turn. You can do this before, during, or after your movement.
- If you use a yurt to skip a stop, you were not at that stop. You cannot take tributes from it.
- You can take more than one tribute piece from one province.

There are 3 types of tribute piece. Each type lets you do one extra action:

| Tribute piece | Extra action |
|---|---|
| sword | Attack cities. |
| yurt | Build your yurts on the map. |
| coin | 2 coins buy 1 upgrade. |

You can use a tribute piece at once, or keep it for later turns. After you use a tribute piece, put it back in the supply.

### 7.3 Use the Khan

- This action is mandatory. Do it when the column you activate has the Khan icon.
- Move the Khan pawn to a place with the Khan icon: one of the 4 Khan provinces or the upgrade area.
- The Khan cannot stay where he is.
- At the start of the game, the Khan is off the board. The first player to use him can choose any of the 5 places. After that, each use must move him to a different place.

**Khan in a province:** Put 1 tribute piece in the Khan province and in its 2 adjacent provinces. The icon of each province sets the type. Add the piece even if the province already has pieces. The maximum is 3 pieces in each province.

**Khan in the upgrade area:** Show new upgrades in the empty spaces. Before you fill the spaces, look for a coin icon and a sword icon. If a space with one of these icons is empty, take that tribute piece from the supply.

### 7.4 Recover your activation pieces

When you activate the white column, take back all your activation pieces. This includes the piece that you just used. All columns are free again in your next turn.

## 8. Extra actions (p. 10 to 14)

You can do extra actions at any time in your turn. You must have the resources, the right location, or both.

### 8.1 Attack cities with swords

- You can attack a city if it has treasures and you made a stop adjacent to it during this turn.
- The first treasure from a city costs 1 sword.
- Each additional treasure from the same city in the same turn costs 2 swords.

If you take the last treasure of a city, you conquer it. Then:

1. Show the next city piece from the pile. Put 4 random treasures on the new city. If the pile is empty, skip this step.
2. Put one of your yurts in the center of the conquered city. This yurt does not help your movement. But it counts for the yurt majority of its region at the end of the game.

### 8.2 Build yurts

- Each yurt tribute piece lets you build one yurt.
- Build on a stop that you made during your movement in this turn. The stop must have no yurt. Another pawn can be on the stop.
- Each stop holds only one yurt. Exception: a double stop holds 2 yurts, and both can be of the same player.
- Karakorum cannot have yurts.
- Your yurts let you skip a stop when you move. But you cannot take tributes or do actions at a stop that you skip.
- When you have no more yurts, you cannot build.

At the end of the game, the players with the most and the 2nd most yurts in each region get votes (see Section 14.1).

### 8.3 Buy upgrades with coins

- At any time in your turn, pay 2 coins to buy 1 upgrade. Take one from the upgrade area, or take the top upgrade of the pile.
- Put the upgrade in one of the 3 colored columns of your player board. The background color of the upgrade must match the column color.
- Fill each column from the bottom to the top.
- You cannot use an upgrade in the turn that you buy it.
- After you buy, move the other upgrades in the upgrade area to the right. Do not show new upgrades. New upgrades come out only when a player puts the Khan in the upgrade area.
- When you complete a row of 3 upgrades on your board, you get 1 free upgrade for the white column. Take one from the upgrade area or from the top of the pile. The white column has no color rule.
- Each colored column matches one region, by color. At the end of the game, the number of upgrades in a column sets the votes for that region (see Section 14.1).

### 8.4 Give treasures to counselors

- When you make a stop at a counselor during your turn, you can give treasures to that counselor for votes.
- There are 7 counselor stops: 6 double stops around the map and 1 in Karakorum.
- You can give treasures to a counselor after another player takes its bonus piece.

| Counselors | Treasures for one delivery | Votes |
|---|---|---|
| 5 counselors | 2 treasures of the same specific type | 1 |
| 1 counselor | 3 treasures of any type | 1 |
| Karakorum | 3 treasures of the specific types on Karakorum | 2 |

The first player who gives treasures to a counselor also gets the bonus piece of that counselor.

The rulebook does not say where delivered treasures go. **[unclear]**

### 8.5 Complete open counselor cards

- When you meet the goal of an open counselor card during your turn, you get 1 vote.
- Remove the card from the game. Do not show a new card.
- Only the first player who meets the goal gets the vote.

### 8.6 Get votes

- Each time you get a vote, move your player token 1 space on the Kurultai track.
- Also move the neutral token 1 space. The neutral token moves each time any player gets a vote. It shows how full the council is.
- When the neutral token reaches a set number, the end of the game starts (see Section 14).

## 9. Upgrades (p. 16 to 17)

Pay 2 coins to buy an upgrade. You cannot use it in the turn that you buy it.

| Upgrade | Quantity | Effect when you activate its column |
|---|---|---|
| virtual coin | 8 | Counts as 1 coin in this turn. You do not get a coin piece. |
| virtual treasure | 1 of each treasure type (5) | Counts as 1 treasure of its type in this turn. You do not get a treasure piece. Use it for 1 delivery only, with at least 1 real treasure. Or use it for open counselor cards. |
| virtual sword | 8 | Counts as 1 sword in this turn. You do not get a sword piece. |
| virtual yurt | 8 | Counts as 1 yurt tribute piece in this turn. You do not get a yurt tribute piece. |
| fist | 6 | 1 extra action to take a tribute piece from the map. |
| two horses | 9 | 2 extra moves. |

You can have many upgrades of the same type in one column. For example, 2 virtual coin upgrades in one column let you buy an upgrade for free.

## 10. Bonus pieces (p. 17)

You get a bonus piece when you are the first player to give treasures to a counselor on the map. After you use it, turn it face down and keep it. You cannot use it again.

| Bonus piece | Effect |
|---|---|
| throne | Get 1 extra vote at once on the Kurultai track. |
| swords | Get 2 sword tribute pieces. |
| coins | Get 2 coin tribute pieces. |
| yurts | Get 2 yurt tribute pieces. |
| horses and fist | Do 3 extra moves and 1 extra action to take a tribute piece. |
| fists | Do 2 extra actions to take tribute pieces. |
| chests | Take 3 random treasures from the treasure pile. Keep 1 and discard the others. |

## 11. Heirs and special abilities (p. 6, p. 20)

Each player board shows one heir with one special ability. Each ability lets the player break a game rule in a different way.

The full text of each ability is on the player boards, not in the rulebook. The rulebook gives only the Tolui text and some notes. **[unclear]**

**Tolui** (text on the board): At the start of the game, put 1 yurt on Karakorum. This yurt is in no region. You can build yurts on stops adjacent to your pawn.

- The Karakorum yurt does not count for influence in any region. It helps only your movement.
- The Karakorum yurt counts for 2 open counselor cards: "6 yurts on the map" and "10 yurts on the map".
- You can build a yurt on an adjacent stop even if an opponent pawn is on it.

**Altani:**

- Only one of your pawns can do actions. The other pawn cannot attack cities, build yurts, or give treasures, even if it stops.
- A treasure or tribute piece that one pawn gets in a round can be used by the other pawn in the next round.

**Chagatai:**

- You can keep the sword that you get for later turns.

**Jochi:**

- When you complete the 1st row of upgrades, you do not get a free upgrade. The space in the white column is already full.

**Ogedei:**

- You can also use coin upgrades to take a treasure from a city.
- If a city has 2 or more treasures, you can use a coin to take the 1st one. Then, if you are adjacent to the city, you can use a sword to take the next one.

## 12. Open counselor cards (p. 18)

In each game, 4 open counselor cards are face up. When you meet the goal of a card, remove it from the game and get 1 vote on the Kurultai track. Do not show a new card.

The first player to:

1. have 4 swords (you do not spend them).
2. have 6 yurts on the map.
3. have 10 yurts on the map.
4. have 4 treasures (you do not spend them).
5. have 5 different treasures (you do not spend them).
6. have 1 yurt in each region.
7. have 2 yurts in each region.
8. get 2 bonus pieces.
9. get 3 bonus pieces.
10. conquer 2 cities.
11. conquer 3 cities.
12. complete a row of upgrades.
13. have 2 columns with 2 upgrades each.
14. have 4 upgrades in one column.

## 13. Secret counselor cards (p. 19)

Each player gets 2 cards at the start of the game and keeps 1. At the end of the game, the card gives 0 to 3 votes. The number of votes depends on how well you met its goal.

| Card | Goal | 1 vote | 2 votes | 3 votes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Viajante (Traveler) | Have a group of connected yurts. The group can branch. | 3 yurts | 6 yurts | 8 yurts |
| Soldado (Soldier) | Have upgrades. | 4 | 7 | 10 |
| Colecionador (Collector) | Have upgrades with different symbols. Treasure symbols count as different. | 4 | 6 | 7 |
| Sacerdote (Priest) | Have pairs of upgrades with the same symbol. Two pairs can be equal. Different treasure symbols do not count as equal. | 1 pair | 2 pairs | 4 pairs |
| Governador (Governor) | Have regions with at least 2 yurts and 2 upgrades in the matching column. | 1 region | 2 regions | 3 regions |
| Bajulador (Flatterer) | Have Khan provinces with at least 2 adjacent yurts. | 2 provinces | 3 provinces | 4 provinces |
| Prefeito (Mayor) | Have cities with at least 2 adjacent yurts. Yurts inside cities do not count as adjacent. | 2 cities | 3 cities | 4 cities |
| Cidadão (Citizen) | Have cities with at least 1 adjacent yurt. Yurts inside cities do not count as adjacent. | 3 cities | 6 cities | 8 cities |
| Cavaleiro (Rider) | Have upgrades with a horse symbol or a fist symbol. | 2 | 4 | 5 |
| Mensageiro (Messenger) | Have at least 1 yurt on double stops. Karakorum does not count. | 2 stops | 4 stops | 6 stops |

## 14. End of the game (p. 14 to 16)

The end of the game starts when the neutral token reaches this number of votes:

| Players | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Votes | 10 | 14 | 16 | 17 |

The end also starts when players conquer all cities. This is rare.

When the end starts:

1. Finish the current round. The other players play until the turn of the first player.
2. Play one more full round. Each player plays one last turn.
3. After this round, the game ends.

Then count the end-of-game votes. There are 2 types: influence over the 3 regions, and secret counselor cards.

### 14.1 Influence over the 3 regions

1. Count the yurts of each player in each region.
2. In each region, the player with the most yurts and the player with the 2nd most yurts get votes.
3. The number of votes depends on the number of upgrades in the column that matches the region.

| Upgrades in the column | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Votes for the most yurts | 3 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Votes for the 2nd most yurts | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 0 |

- The base actions printed on each column do not count as upgrades.
- Upgrades in the white column do not count, whatever their color.
- If players tie for the most yurts, all of them are 1st. Nobody is 2nd.
- If players tie for the 2nd most yurts, all of them are 2nd.

Example (p. 15): In China, you are 3rd in yurts, so you get 0 votes. In Persia, you are 2nd and have 3 upgrades in the matching column, so you get 2 votes. In Russia, you tie for the most yurts and have 2 upgrades in the matching column, so you get 2 votes.

### 14.2 Secret counselor cards

Show your secret counselor card. It gives 0 to 3 extra votes (see Section 13).

### 14.3 Winner

1. The player with the most votes wins.
2. If there is a tie, the tied player with the most undelivered treasures wins.
3. If there is still a tie, the tied player nearest to Karakorum wins.
4. If there is still a tie, the tied players share the victory and govern the Mongol Empire together.

## 15. Open questions for the group

Settle these points before you design the model:

- The map data: the list of stops, double stops, routes, provinces, cities, and which of them are adjacent. The rulebook shows the map only as a picture.
- The base actions and colors of each column on each player board.
- The full special ability of each heir.
- The 5 treasure types and which counselor asks for which type.
- Where delivered treasures go (the supply or out of the game).
- The positions of the icons in the upgrade area (5 spaces, with coin and sword icons).
